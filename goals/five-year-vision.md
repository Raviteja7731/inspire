### Long-Term North Star (Written Late 2024)
> "Build things that outlive the quarterly hype cycle. Protect time for the people who loved you before anyone knew your name. Become extraordinarily good at your craft while keeping your spirit soft, curious, and kind."
— **North Star Statement**  
*Date: 2024-12-30*  
*Source: Annual Personal Review*

---

### Three Anchor Commitments for the Next Decade
1. **Financial Independence with Modest Needs**: Build assets that provide sovereign control over time, rather than escalating lifestyle costs to match income.
2. **Deep Work over Wide Flattery**: Aim to write, design, and engineer things that a handful of thoughtful people cherish for ten years, rather than things that ten thousand people forget in ten minutes.
3. **Physical Vitality as a Baseline**: Arrive in my forties with strong knees, cardiovascular resilience, and zero back pain through consistent daily lifting, walking, and clean nutrition.

*Note to self: Don't let urgent trivialities swallow the quiet important truths.*
