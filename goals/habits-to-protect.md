### The Golden First Hour (Morning Protocol)
*Source: Habit Ledger 2025*  
*Date: 2025-02-15*

No screens, no notifications, no email until:
- 10 minutes of natural sunlight outside
- A tall glass of cold water with a pinch of sea salt
- 20 minutes of movement or gentle stretching
- 30 minutes of deep focused work on my primary creative project

When I protect this first hour, the entire day belongs to me. When I surrender it, I spend the whole day reacting to other people's priorities.

### The Evening Shutdown Ritual
*Source: Habit Ledger 2025*  
*Date: 2025-05-18*

At 9:30 PM:
1. Close all browser tabs and dock laptop in drawer.
2. Write down tomorrow's 3 primary tasks on a physical index card.
3. Switch off harsh overhead LEDs; turn on warm low lamps.
4. Read physical books or write longhand in the journal.
