### Goal: Ship the 50-Page Novella / Long Essay Collection
*Status: In Progress • Target: Spring 2027*  
*Source: Black Hardcover Moleskine*  
*Date: 2025-03-01*

Write 500 words three mornings a week. Don't edit while drafting. Let the characters be flawed and allow the dialogue to breathe. The goal is completion, not perfection.

### Goal: Design and Hand-Craft a Walnut Reading Stand
*Status: Committed*  
*Source: Woodworking Project Notebook*  
*Date: 2025-06-14*

Learn basic mortise and tenon joinery. Build a tilted desk stand from black walnut with brass pegs. Work with physical materials where undo doesn't exist, to cultivate patience and physical precision.

### Goal: 100 Days of Analog Photography
*Status: Planned*  
*Source: Visual Diary 2026*  
*Date: 2026-01-10*

Shoot only 36 exposures per month on medium-format or 35mm film. No burst shooting, no instant LCD preview. Learn to look at shadows, gestures, and negative space with patient scrutiny before pressing the shutter.
