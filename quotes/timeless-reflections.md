### Marcus Aurelius on the Present Moment
> "Never let the future disturb you. You will meet it, if you have to, with the same weapons of reason which today arm you against the present."
— **Marcus Aurelius**, *Meditations (Book VII)*  
*Saved: 2023-03-12*  
*Note to self: Read this whenever you feel your mind spiraling into hypothetical next-month anxieties. The tools you have right now are enough.*

---

### Mary Oliver on Wonder and Daily Life
> "Instructions for living a life: Pay attention. Be astonished. Tell about it."
— **Mary Oliver**, *Red Bird*  
*Saved: 2023-08-19*  
*Note to self: The antidote to burnout isn't just rest; it's noticing ordinary beauty before it slips away.*

---

### James Clear on Identity and Habit
> "You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems."
— **James Clear**, *Atomic Habits*  
*Saved: 2024-01-05*  
*Note to self: Stop obsessing over the outcome. Just protect tomorrow morning's 45-minute writing block.*

---

### Rainer Maria Rilke on Living the Questions
> "Be patient toward all that is unsolved in your heart and try to love the questions themselves, like locked rooms and like books that are now written in a very foreign tongue. Do not now seek the answers, which cannot be given you because you would not be able to live them. And the point is, to live everything. Live the questions now."
— **Rainer Maria Rilke**, *Letters to a Young Poet*  
*Saved: 2024-05-22*  
*Note to self: It's okay not to have the whole next chapter planned out. Trust the unfolding.*

---

### Viktor Frankl on Meaning and Response
> "Between stimulus and response there is a space. In that space is our power to choose our response. In our response lies our growth and our freedom."
— **Viktor E. Frankl**, *Man's Search for Meaning*  
*Saved: 2024-11-15*  
*Note to self: Take three slow breaths before replying to any frustrating message.*

---

### Alan Watts on the Muddy Water
> "Muddy water is best cleared by leaving it alone."
— **Alan Watts**, *The Way of Zen*  
*Saved: 2025-02-10*  
*Note to self: Sometimes trying harder to solve emotional tension is what keeps it churned up. Go for a walk.*
