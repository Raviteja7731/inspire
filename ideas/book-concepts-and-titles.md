### Concept: *The Cartography of Quiet Places*
*Source: Airport Notebook*  
*Date: 2025-01-20*

A book investigating the remaining acoustic sanctuaries of the world—places where human industrial noise is absent for at least 15 continuous minutes. Follow acoustic ecologists, monks in Hokkaido, and park rangers in the Hoh Rain Forest.

### Concept: *The Unfinished House*
*Source: Late night voice memo transcript*  
*Date: 2025-09-12*

An architectural and philosophical memoir exploring why the most vibrant lives leave room for unfinished rooms, empty corners, and projects that are purposefully never finalized. Perfectionism as a form of fear.
