### Experiment: The 30-Day Secondhand Book Challenge
*Source: Idea Jar • Autumn 2024*  
*Date: 2024-11-02*

Spend 30 days reading exclusively books bought from used bookstores for under $5, with previous owners' notes and marginalia left intact. Track what kind of unexpected serendipity comes from reading thoughts annotated by strangers across decades.

### Experiment: The Weekly Silent Walk (No Earbuds)
*Source: Sunday Reflections*  
*Date: 2025-04-06*

Take one 60-minute walk every weekend with absolutely no headphones, podcasts, audiobooks, or calls. Just raw acoustic environment: footsteps crunching on gravel, crows in telephone wires, car tires on wet asphalt, your own breathing. Let thoughts wander without an external voice steering the narrative.

### Experiment: One Physical Letter Every Month
*Source: Desk Note*  
*Date: 2025-10-24*

Buy fountain pen ink and heavy 100gsm cotton stationary. Once a month, write an unprompted, sincere 2-page letter of gratitude to someone who altered the course of your life (an old teacher, a cousin, a former coworker, an old roommate). Expect nothing in return.
