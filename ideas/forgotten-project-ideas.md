### Idea: The Ambient World Compass
*Source: Blue Scribble Notebook • Coffee shop sketch*  
*Date: 2023-10-18*

A minimalist bedside device made of spun brass and matte glass. Instead of showing the time or notifications, it slowly rotates with magnetic heading. Point it toward Kyoto, Reykjavik, or Oaxaca to stream 60 seconds of gentle, uncompressed real-time environmental audio—wind in temple bamboo, waves hitting black basalt, or rain on terracotta tiles. 

No screen, no buttons except volume. A gentle portal to remind you that life is happening everywhere right now.

### Idea: The Five-Year Letter Capsule
*Source: Flight Notes JFK to SFO*  
*Date: 2024-02-14*

A web service where you write a letter to yourself five years in the future, but with a twist: you can only write it on your birthday, and you must include 3 predictions, 1 thing you are currently struggling with, and 1 person you are deeply grateful for.

It stores the entry in encrypted storage and physical print backup, and mails a sealed wax envelope on your exact birthday five years later.

### Idea: "The Museum of Fleeting Moments"
*Source: Walk in the Rain Notes*  
*Date: 2024-09-08*

A micro-podcast or tiny web journal where each entry is under 90 seconds. It captures microscopic human moments that would otherwise be lost: the sound of a train conductor whistling while stamping tickets at dusk, the way grandmother always folded the corner of a newspaper, or the smell of rain hitting hot summer asphalt.
