Woke up at 6:45 AM before the alarm. Heavy spring rain drumming rhythmic, gentle waves against the bedroom glass. 

I didn't reach for my phone. Instead, I stood in the kitchen in wool socks and ground Ethiopian beans by hand. The steam rose in pale curls into the dim gray light. Sat on the window ledge with an open notebook, watching tiny rivulets race down the pane and pool in the garden hydrangea leaves.

No deadlines were pressing in that hour. It reminded me how much wealth is contained in a single quiet morning where nobody expects anything from you, and your only job is to breathe, taste warm coffee, and watch the storm pass.

*Why I want to remember this:* You don't need exotic vacations to feel restored. You just need an hour where you aren't performing for anyone.
