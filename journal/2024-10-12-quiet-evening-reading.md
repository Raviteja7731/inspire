Turned off the overhead lights, clicked on the brass desk lamp, and brewed peppermint tea. Opened Italo Calvino’s *Invisible Cities*.

For the first time in months, I didn't skim. I read every sentence twice, savoring the architecture of the prose. Hours slipped past like water under a bridge. When I looked up, the clock read 11:30 PM, the tea had cooled, and my heart felt unusually peaceful and nourished.

We lose so much by treating reading as an efficiency exercise or something to "consume" at 2x speed. Deep immersion is an act of defiance against a culture that wants our minds fragmented into ten-second shards.
