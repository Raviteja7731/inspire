### Diner Conversation with Elena on Pacing
*Source: Coffee with Elena at The Red Door Diner*  
*Date: 2024-05-19*

Elena looked across the booth over blueberry pancakes and said:
> "Don't measure yourself by how fast you run in someone else's race. Most people who seem to be sprinting ahead are running toward an exhaustion they haven't admitted yet. Find a pace you could keep for thirty years without losing your warmth."

*I wrote this on the back of the diner receipt so I wouldn't forget.*

### Grandfather's Rule for Hard Decisions
*Source: Summer porch conversation in Vermont*  
*Date: 2023-07-22*

When faced with two paths and both seem terrifying:
> "Pick the choice that enlarges your capacity to love and learn, rather than the choice that protects your ego. The ego's favorite trick is convincing you that playing small keeps you safe."
