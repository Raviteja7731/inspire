### On Attention as the Rarest Currency
*Source: Margin notes in Simone Weil’s Gravity and Grace*  
*Date: 2024-06-03*

Attention is the rarest and purest form of generosity. When you give someone your undivided presence—not looking past their shoulder, not checking your wrist, not preparing your counterargument—you are giving them a piece of your unrepeatable finite existence on earth. Never squander attention on rage-bait.

### The Awkward Intermediate Stage of Any Craft
*Source: Studio Note stuck to wall monitor*  
*Date: 2024-12-11*

Every meaningful project passes through a stage where it looks completely broken and amateurish. Beginners mistake this phase for failure and abandon the work. Professionals know this awkward phase is simply the necessary structural scaffolding before coherence arrives. Stay in the room.

### On Small Daily Rituals
*Source: Yellow Legal Pad*  
*Date: 2025-07-29*

We think our lives are made of big dramatic decisions (changing jobs, moving cities, marriages). But day-to-day emotional well-being is overwhelmingly determined by miniature physical textures: the weight of your favorite ceramic mug, whether you made the bed, five minutes of stretching, the temperature of the shower, and how you speak to people who serve your food.
